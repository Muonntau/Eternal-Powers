# Concord & Covenant campaign update

Version 3 adds a separate faction story to the manual-based regional contracts.
Existing characters keep all their progression and begin the new story at its
first mission. New characters choose their lineage, form and native starting
power in the character creator.

## Two faction campaigns

Angels join the Concord of Dawn in **The Living Concord**. Demons join the
Ashbound Covenant in **The Unbound Pact**. Each campaign has three acts and
12 authored missions, faction-specific dialogue, recorded decisions, a
final choice and a persistent ending/title. Mission settlement awards native
Karma exactly once and records a receipt. Reopening dialogue, reloading or
traveling cannot replay a claim. The separate 117 regional contracts remain.

The story begins in Sacred Groves, then crosses Prismatic Waterfalls, Ethereal
Gardens, Celestial Observatories, Infernal Gates, Suffering Labyrinths and the
City of Stars. It teaches preparation and training, resonance, rune alignment,
pulse timing, cultivation, carrying a spark and stabilizing a shelter prism.
Actual casts and collected results provide progress. A completed challenge in
an upcoming story region is remembered and accepted when its mission arrives.

The current objective appears directly below the map on the right. It states
the task, region, act and step, changes to a return instruction for settlement,
and provides a real walking route. Travel is refused during conflict or cast
preparation. The campaign panel shows all 12 missions and their status.

## Reactive world sites

All 39 regions contain six physically placed, walkable challenge sites:

| Site | Interaction | Material output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lattice Resonator | Resolve two powers | 2 crystal |
| Harmonic Rune Gate | Align and cast visible notes 1 → 3 → 2; wrong notes slip one step | 2 stone, 1 crystal |
| Echo Well | Resolve two pulses at least one second apart, within the power-adjusted response window | 2 residue |
| Aether Relay | Charge a spark and carry it to its ring within 30 seconds | 3 wood |
| Shelter Prism | Select and stabilize three different facets | 3 stone |
| Echo Bloom | Resolve a power to cultivate its seed | 3 herb |

Every local named power can drive a site, through the normal preparation,
Essence, cooldown, range and line-of-effect checks. Learned cross-affinity
techniques also work. World rituals retain their world-project execution flow.
Casts revalidate the target before spending resources. Sites animate their
state in the world and expose their instructions and progress in the purpose
window. Collection is explicit and atomic. Materials replenish after 75 game
seconds; repeat harvesting produces no lifetime Karma. Carry challenges and
collection cooldowns survive save/reload and are rebased to the server clock.

## Purpose windows and markers

Envoys provide story decisions, rewards and guidance. The stranded courier
provides a faction-specific choice. Keepers connect standing/trials, the
campaign and sanctuary; artisans connect supplies, crafting and equipment;
quartermasters connect barter, commissions and party support. Training targets,
resources, crossings, caches, portals and landmarks explain their purpose and
expose the corresponding action. Creature windows retain exact material needs,
authoritative offering, bonding and companion controls.

Interaction actions are checked again at commitment. Out-of-range targets offer
an approach route. Only available story dialogue uses an exclamation point;
current story targets use a diamond and reward hand-ins use a check. Nearby
services use subdued service symbols. Personal actors are hidden from other
travelers in the renderer, map and compact multiplayer snapshots, and foreign
interactions are rejected by the server.

## Art direction

Eight original generated landscape plates cover verdant sanctuaries, prismatic
waterfalls, cosmic deserts, civic cities, celestial terraces, an observatory,
an infernal citadel and the City of Stars. Region cards and faction banners
use these plates. Reactive world structures use animated geometric crystal,
petal, rune, ripple and orbital forms alongside the existing expanded region
architecture. The interface uses legible navy panels, cyan/violet light,
restrained gold reward cues, clear hierarchy and responsive purpose windows.
